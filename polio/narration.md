## Original supplied Hinglish script

Polio wapas aa gaya hai, lekin kyun?

Global campaign ne wild polio ko sirf do deshon tak rok diya hai. Par conflict aur poor healthcare access se full coverage mushkil hai, aur rare detections dikhate hain ki hamari progress abhi bhi fragile hai.

Ek aur badi problem hai. Immunity gaps ki wajah se, oral vaccine ka weakened virus phail sakta hai aur rare cases mein mutate hokar wapas paralysis karta hai. Ye wild cases se zyada hain, aur polio ka lamba incubation period isey unvaccinated logon mein hafton tak undetected phailne deta hai.

Good news yeh hai ki ab kam mutate hone wali vaccines ki 2 billion doses di ja chuki hain. Par polio ko end karne ke liye humein war zones samet, har chhoote hue bache tak pahunchna hoga.

Ek single case bhi outbreak ka sign hai, isliye real-time surveillance zaroori hai. Ab tak 20 million bache paralyzed hone se bache hain aur 1.6 million lives save hui hain. Lekin agar humne momentum khoya, toh laakhon new cases ka khatra phir se khada ho jayega.
